Definition

Let TT be a topological space closed under arbitrary intersections (i.e. an Alexandrov topology, not to be confused with Alexandrov space), a topological interpretation (of propositional subtractive logic) is a function ⟦⋅⟧\llbracket \cdot \rrbracket from sentences to opens of TT satisfying:

⟦A⟧={T ifA=⊤ ∅ ifA=⊥ ⟦B⟧∪⟦C⟧ ifA=B∧C ⟦B⟧∩⟦C⟧ ifA=B∨C Ext(⟦B⟧∖⟦C⟧) ifA=B⇒C Int(⟦B⟧∖⟦C⟧) ifA=B−C \llbracket A \rrbracket = \begin{cases} T & \,\text{if}\, A = \top\\ \emptyset & \,\text{if}\, A = \bot \\ \llbracket B \rrbracket \cup \llbracket C \rrbracket & \,\text{if}\, A = B \wedge C \\ \llbracket B \rrbracket \cap \llbracket C \rrbracket & \,\text{if}\, A = B \vee C \\ \mathrm{Ext}(\llbracket B \rrbracket \setminus \llbracket C \rrbracket) & \,\text{if}\, A = B \implies C \\ \mathrm{Int}(\llbracket B \rrbracket \setminus \llbracket C \rrbracket) & \,\text{if}\, A = B - C \end{cases}

and we say that A⊢BA \vdash B is true for this interpretation if ⟦A⟧⊆⟦B⟧\llbracket A \rrbracket \subseteq \llbracket B \rrbracket
