Reviews and overviews

Anomaly cancellation and the gravitational Chern–Simons coupling

For the physical origin of C 3∧I 8C_3\wedge I_8, compare the type IIA one-loop calculation with inflow for a magnetic M5-brane. The corresponding superspace problem is described below.

  • Cumrun Vafa, Edward Witten, A One-Loop Test of String Duality, Nuclear Physics B 447 (1995) 261–270. The type IIA torus amplitude and the resulting BB-field tadpole. arXiv:hep-th/9505053

  • Mike Duff, James Liu, Ruben Minasian, Eleven Dimensional Origin of String/String Duality: A One Loop Test, Nuclear Physics B 452 (1995) 261–282. §3 derives the curvature source from the five-brane anomaly. arXiv:hep-th/9506126

  • Petr Hořava, Edward Witten, Eleven-Dimensional Supergravity on a Manifold with Boundary, Nuclear Physics B 475 (1996) 94–114. The discussion following (1.14) relates the two derivations. arXiv:hep-th/9603142, doi

  • Edward Witten, On Flux Quantization in M-Theory and the Effective Action, Journal of Geometry and Physics 22 (1997) 1–13. §3 treats the eight-dimensional characteristic number and membrane charge. §§4–5 give the global phase analysis. arXiv:hep-th/9609122, doi

  • Daniel Freed, Jeffrey Harvey, Ruben Minasian, Gregory Moore, Gravitational Anomaly Cancellation for M-Theory Fivebranes, Advances in Theoretical and Mathematical Physics 2 (1998) 601–618. Treats the residual normal-bundle anomaly and the contribution of the cubic bulk coupling. arXiv:hep-th/9803205

  • Emanuel Diaconescu, Daniel Freed, Gregory Moore, The M-theory 3-form and E 8E_8 gauge theory (2003 preprint). Gives a global Chern–Simons phase and an integral electric charge for the CC-field. arXiv:hep-th/0312069
