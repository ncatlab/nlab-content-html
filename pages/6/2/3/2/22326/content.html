Higher curvature corrections to supergravity

Higher curvature corrections to D=11D=11 supergravity

References on higher curvature corrections and their flux and fermion completions in 11-dimensional supergravity, viewed as the low-energy effective field theory of M-theory. The references are grouped by method and application; results for selected field sectors or scattering amplitudes are distinguished from analyses of the full superspace deformation problem.

Reviews and overviews

  • Paul Howe: R 4R^4 terms in supergravity and M-theory, contribution to Deserfest: A Celebration of the Life and Works of Stanley Deser (preprint 2004) 137–149 [arXiv:hep-th/0408177, inspire:657136]

  • Mehmet Ozkan, Yi Pang, Ergin Sezgin, Higher Derivative Supergravities in Diverse Dimensions, Physics Reports 1086 (2024) 1–95 [doi:10.1016/j.physrep.2024.07.002, arXiv:2401.08945]

Anomaly cancellation and the gravitational Chern-Simons coupling

On the origin and consistency of the coupling C 3∧I 8C_3\wedge I_8 to the I8 term, its relation to M5-brane anomaly cancellation, and the gravitational contribution to C-field flux quantization:

  • Cumrun Vafa, Edward Witten, A One-Loop Test of String Duality, Nuclear Physics B 447 (1995) 261–270 [arXiv:hep-th/9505053]

  • Mike Duff, James Liu, Ruben Minasian, Eleven Dimensional Origin of String/String Duality: A One Loop Test, Nuclear Physics B 452 (1995) 261–282 [arXiv:hep-th/9506126]

  • Edward Witten: On Flux Quantization in M-Theory and the Effective Action, Journal of Geometry and Physics 22 (1997) 1–13 [arXiv:hep-th/9609122]

  • Daniel Freed, Jeffrey Harvey, Ruben Minasian, Gregory Moore: Gravitational Anomaly Cancellation for M-Theory Fivebranes, Advances in Theoretical and Mathematical Physics 2 (1998) 601–618 [arXiv:hep-th/9803205]

For the superspace analysis of this coupling, see below.

Superspace deformation theory

Relaxed torsion constraints and spinorial cohomology

Via deformations of superspace torsion constraints and the cohomology controlling supersymmetric deformations:

  • Martin Cederwall, Ulf Gran, Mikkel Nielsen, Bengt Nilsson, Generalised 11-dimensional supergravity, in A. Semikhatov, M. Vasiliev and V. Zaikin (eds.), Proceedings of Quantization, Gauge Theory & Strings, Moscow (2000_ [arXiv:hep-th/0010042]

  • Martin Cederwall, Bengt Nilsson, Dimitrios Tsimpis, Spinorial cohomology and maximally supersymmetric theories, JHEP 02 (2002) 009 [doi:10.1088/1126-6708/2002/02/009, arXiv:hep-th/0110069]

  • Dimitrios Tsimpis, 11D supergravity at 𝒪(ℓ 3)\mathcal{O}(\ell^3), JHEP 10 (2004) 046 [arXiv:hep-th/0407271, doi:10.1088/1126-6708/2004/10/046]

    Includes the relation between the order-ℓ 3\ell^3 deformation and a shift of four-form flux quantization.

  • Martin Cederwall, Ulf Gran, Bengt Nilsson, Dimitrios Tsimpis, Supersymmetric Corrections to Eleven-Dimensional Supergravity, JHEP 05 (2005) 052 [arXiv:hep-th/0409107, doi:10.1088/1126-6708/2005/05/052]

    Solves the geometric superspace Bianchi identities to first order in a general dimension-zero torsion deformation.

See also Howe-Tsimpis below for the four-form formulation of the deformation problem.

Anomaly-corrected Bianchi identities and the superform action principle

On the supersymmetric completion of C 3∧I 8C_3\wedge I_8, the corresponding deformation of the seven-form Bianchi identity, and its analysis using spinorial cohomology and the superform action principle:

  • Paul Howe, Dimitrios Tsimpis, On higher-order corrections in M theory, JHEP 09 (2003) 038 [arXiv:hep-th/0305129, doi:10.1088/1126-6708/2003/09/038]

    In particular, Sections 4-6: four-form deformations, the anomaly-corrected seven-form Bianchi identity, and reconstruction of the lowest-dimensional superspace components.

  • Bertrand Souères, Dimitrios Tsimpis, Action principle and the supersymmetrization of Chern-Simons terms in eleven-dimensional supergravity, Physical Review D 95 (2017) 026013 [arXiv:1612.02021, doi:10.1103/PhysRevD.95.026013]

    In particular, Sections 4-5 and Appendix C: the order-ℓ 6\ell^6 invariant, the τ\tau-exactness problem for the purely spinorial component of I 8I_8 (denoted X 8X_8 there, with different normalization), and integrability of the deformation.

  • Bertrand Souères, Supergravities in Superspace, doctoral thesis, Lyon (2018) [tel:01998725, pdf]

An LLM generated proof of existence of higher curvature corrected superspace 11D supergravity, so far to second order in the I 8 I_8 -deformation of the G 7 G_7 -Bianchi identity:

  • nLab materials: Existence Proof of Curvature Corrected 11D Superspace Supergravity (2026-11-07) [db]

  • Yoshifumi Hyakutake, Sachiko Ogushi, Higher Derivative Corrections to Eleven Dimensional Supergravity via Local Supersymmetry, JHEP 02 (2006) 068 [arXiv:hep-th/0601092, doi:10.1088/1126-6708/2006/02/068]

  • Yoshifumi Hyakutake, Toward the Determination of R 3F 2R^3F^2 Terms in M-theory, Progress of Theoretical Physics 118 (2007) 109–119 [arXiv:hep-th/0703154, doi:10.1143/PTP.118.109]

    Analysis of the (DF) 4(DF)^4 sector and associated fermion bilinears by local supersymmetry.

Generalized Lichnerowicz formula

Via higher-derivative corrections to the spinorial differential operators entering supersymmetry transformations and a generalized Lichnerowicz formula:

  • André Coimbra, Ruben Minasian, M-theoretic Lichnerowicz formula and supersymmetry, JHEP 10 (2019) 036 [arXiv:1705.04330, doi:10.1007/JHEP10(2019)036]

    Includes curvature corrections and applications to G 2G_2 compactifications; the higher-derivative flux completion is discussed as a further problem in Section 6.1.
